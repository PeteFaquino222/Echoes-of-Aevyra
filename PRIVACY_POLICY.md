**Echoes of Aevyra — Privacy Policy**
Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-08-11

This Policy describes what the Bot processes when you play Echoes of Aevyra.

**1. Who controls the data**
Each Discord server may run its own Bot instance. The **instance operator** (whoever hosts the Bot process and its data files) controls that instance’s stored game data. This Policy describes typical processing for a standard Echoes of Aevyra deployment.

**2. Data we process**
The Bot may store and process:
• **Discord identifiers** — your Discord user ID; display name may appear in public messages the Bot sends
• **Game profile** — character names, race, gender, class, backstory text, level, XP, HP, gold, region, stats
• **Inventory & progression** — items, gear, cards, pets, quest progress, achievements, reputation, guild membership
• **Optional AI chat context** — recent messages used only to narrate scenes (if AI features are enabled by the operator)
• **Technical logs** — errors and operational logs on the host machine (operator-controlled)

We do **not** intentionally collect email addresses, phone numbers, payment card numbers, or government IDs through the Bot.

**3. How data is obtained**
• Directly from Discord when you use slash commands and buttons
• Generated by gameplay (loot, combat results, quest updates)
• Optionally from third-party AI/image providers if the operator configured API keys

**4. Why we process it**
• To run the game (characters, combat, quests, shops, guilds, pets, cards)
• To show public or ephemeral Discord messages you request
• To improve stability (error handling) on the operator’s side

**5. Storage**
Typical self-hosted setups store JSON (or similar) files on the operator’s computer or server. Retention lasts until the operator deletes the data or you request deletion through them. Discord also processes message content under Discord’s own Privacy Policy when the Bot posts in channels.

**6. Sharing**
• **Public Discord channels** — many game actions post publicly so other members can see outcomes
• **Ephemeral replies** — some commands are visible only to you
• **Third parties** — if AI narration or image generation is enabled, prompts may be sent to providers such as Groq, Pollinations, or other services the operator configures; those providers process data under their own terms
• We do not sell your personal data

**7. Children**
The Bot is not directed at children under the age required by Discord or local law. Operators should not knowingly store data from users who should not be on Discord.

**8. Your choices**
• Stop using the Bot at any time
• Ask your **server moderators / Bot operator** to delete your player data from that instance
• Use Discord’s privacy settings and leave servers where the Bot is present
• Skip optional AI features by not using AI-related commands if the operator exposes them

**9. Security**
Operators should protect host machines and tokens. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Protect your Discord account with a strong password and 2FA.

**10. Changes**
This Policy may be updated; the version and effective date above will change when material updates occur. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.

**11. Contact**
For data access or deletion on a specific instance, contact the Bot operator or server staff for that Discord server—not Discord Support for game-save files.
